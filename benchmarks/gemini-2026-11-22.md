# Wingman model comparison · 22 November 2026

## Main

**Haiku 5.5 at provider defaults remains a reasonable first choice for conversation and tools.** On a repeat with alternating request order, it completed 21 of 24 tasks correctly in a median 2.36 seconds. Explicit adaptive-low completed 19 in 2.18 seconds. The initial runs scored 16 and 19 respectively. A two-task lead on one repeat leaves room for either ordering on new requests.

Flash-Lite was the fastest Gemini here (2.54 seconds, 17/24). Flash and Argon each scored 19/24, taking 5.50 and 9.69 seconds. All three Gemini 4 models answered the 15 tasks with determinate grades across all models correctly. Several other tasks requested information outside the simulated tools’ recorded responses, which made those particular answers unverifiable.

Players asked about navigation, trade, cargo arithmetic, key bindings, radio controls, saved files and provider status. Each model received the same tool definitions and worked through its own sequence of calls against recorded responses. A full pass required the correct action or answer, including relevant units, timestamps and uncertainty. Every task had been solved in an earlier run. Partial and unverified answers stayed in the denominator.

“Provider default” leaves thinking and effort to the provider. The adaptive-low route explicitly requested low effort through Anthropic’s native API. The repeat alternated which route went first on 12 pairs apiece. The trials used real inference, simulated tools and blind AI grading with a second review. Timings cover the full tool workflow and exclude voice, game input and Wingman scheduling. The November 9 local result is included for hardware context.

The charts show estimated USD per **1,000 tasks of the same mix**. Gemini Main costs use the amounts OpenRouter reported. Haiku estimates use token counts, with uncached input assumed for the compatible endpoint. Grading and local electricity are excluded.
